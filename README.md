Sales Performance Analysis Dashboard | Power BI
1. Project Overview

This is a practice project developed to analysis sales performance using Microsoft Excel,Power Query & Power BI.
The dashboard provides an overview of business performance through revenue, gross profit, profit margin, order volume, and quantity sold.
The objective is to explore sales trends, compare geographical performance, and understand how different sales channels and customer types contribute to business results.

Project Status: Practice Project

2. Business Problem

This project explores questions such as:

1.How much revenue and gross profit does the business generate?
2.How does sales performance change over time?
3.Which countries generate the highest revenue?
4.Which products contribute most to order volume?
5.How do different sales channels perform?
6.Which customer types contribute most to revenue?

3. Project Objectives

1.Analyse overall sales performance.
2.Monitor key business KPIs.
3.Identify monthly revenue and gross profit trends.
4.Compare country-level performance.
5.Evaluate sales channel contribution.
6.Analyse product and customer-type performance.

7. Dashboard Analysis

A.Monthly Performance
Tracks monthly revenue and gross profit trends.

B.Geographical Performance
Identifies the top five countries by revenue and provides a broader country-level breakdown.

C.Product Performance
Examines the top-selling products based on the selected order metric.

D.Sales Channel Analysis
Compares revenue and gross profit across sales channels.

D.Customer Analysis
Examines revenue contribution by customer type.


8. Interactive Features

The report provides slicers for:

1.Country
2.Month
3.Product Category
4.Quarter

These allow users to explore sales performance across different business dimensions.
